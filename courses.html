Each course is a self-contained site: modules with worked exercises and quizzes, a glossary, and a consolidated bibliography. Work through a course in order or open the module you need on Monday.

Published

  • Design for Manufacturing and Assembly: From Prototype to Production Published

    For mechanical and manufacturing engineers who know CAD and the basic processes but have not yet lived through a supplier DFM review that rejected their part, a tolerance stack-up that failed at production validation, or a ramp that stalled on a hard-to-reach screw.

    Modules: why DFx; the NPI process; process selection; tolerancing; DFM for CNC machining, sheet metal, injection moulding, casting and forging, additive manufacturing, and PCB assembly; design for assembly; design for test; service, reliability, and compliance; materials, finishes, and joining; suppliers and contract manufacturers; cost modeling; a capstone review of a portable 12 V tyre inflator.

    About 90,000 words, 33 diagrams, 134 quiz questions, 148 cited sources. Free, no sign-up.
