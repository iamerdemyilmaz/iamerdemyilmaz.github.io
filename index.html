Courses

Free, practitioner-written courses for engineers with roughly zero to five years of experience, on getting a design made, assembled, tested, and serviced at the volume and cost the business needs.

  • Design for Manufacturing and Assembly

    From prototype to production. Seventeen modules on DFM by process (machining, sheet metal, moulding, casting, additive, PCB assembly), design for assembly, test, and service, tolerancing and process capability, cost modeling, supplier engagement, and a capstone review of a complete product. About 90,000 words, 33 diagrams, 134 quiz questions, and 148 cited sources. Free, no sign-up.

    Start the course

  • In preparation Planned

    Tolerance analysis and GD&T in depth: datum strategy, position and profile in practice, stack-ups across assemblies, and what process capability data does to a tolerance decision.

    New product introduction for engineers who lead it: phase gates, build planning, risk registers, supplier readiness, and the production ramp.
