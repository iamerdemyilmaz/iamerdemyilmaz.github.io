Engineering that survives contact with the factory

I am a senior manufacturing engineer. I write free courses for engineers with roughly zero to five years of experience, on the part of the job that school does not teach: getting a design made, assembled, tested, and serviced at the volume and cost the business needs.

How the courses are written

Every rule of thumb comes with the reason behind it, so you can rebuild the rule when a supplier's numbers differ. Every real-world example is cited to a source you can read yourself, and anything that could not be verified is labelled as a rule of thumb rather than dressed up as data. Units are metric. Examples with invented numbers say so.

The sites are plain HTML with no tracking and no accounts. Quiz results stay in your browser.
